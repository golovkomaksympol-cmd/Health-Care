# Relationships Framework

Relationships are a healthspan input, not a "soft" topic. The Harvard Study of Adult Development (80+ years) puts relationship quality in the top predictors of longevity, alongside genetics. Loneliness and conflict-laden relationships increase mortality at rates comparable to smoking.

Causal chain: relationship quality → cortisol regulation → sleep → cognitive function → quality of decisions → fewer conflicts → better relationships. The loop runs in both directions, but the negative direction runs **faster** (one fight tanks sleep for 1-2 nights).

## The four levels (with their structural vulnerabilities)

Each level has a default protocol and an "Idol" — a failure mode where the wiring gets hijacked by something that looks like success but isn't.

### Level 0: Self ↔ Self (Internal foundation)

Goal: autonomy, stability, efficient resource management of the personality.

- **Meditation** — see `_core/Meditation Framework.md`. Builds the pause between stimulus and response (Libet "Free Won't").
- **Dichotomy of control** (Epictetus / Taleb / Covey): sort every incoming event into 3 buckets to save psychic energy:
  - **Direct control** (own judgments, intentions, actions) → full responsibility, full focus.
  - **Randomness / environment** (luck, mood of others, global events) → ignore. Don't look for causes where there are none.
  - **Sphere of influence** (can shift probability but not guarantee) → do the maximum, accept any outcome without emotional attachment.

**Idol of Pleasure** — system tuned for comfort loses autonomy and adaptability. Any deviation from comfort triggers stress and dysfunction. Without dosed stress, the will atrophies.

### Level 1: Self ↔ Others (Social loop)

Goal: tuning protocols of data exchange (intimacy), managing social capital.

- **Turn Towards** (Gottman) — physical reorientation toward the partner at any bid for connection, however small (sigh, comment, question). Stable couples respond ~86% of the time; unstable ones, 33%. This is the baseline health metric of the channel.
- **Magic Ratio 5:1** (Gottman) — for every 1 negative interaction, minimum 5 positive ones. The brain weighs negativity heavier (negativity bias).
- **Dunbar audit** — quarterly review of the top-5 people in life. Deep connection caps at ~5. Without time investment, entropy.
- **Tail End metric** (Tim Urban) — by adulthood of one's children, ~93% of all lifetime in-person time with them is spent. "40 visits left with parents" is not abstraction, it's a depleting asset. Decisions about social investment should be priced against the remainder, not against the lifespan.

**Idol of Fame** — substituting connection for admiration. Vertical hierarchy instead of horizontal exchange. Trying to impress instead of being present.

### Level 2: Self ↔ Work

Covered separately in `_core/Daily Architecture.md` (Phase 2: High Performance / Deep Work).

**Idols of Money and Power** — focus on outcome instead of process blocks Flow. Trying to control instead of contribute.

### Level 3: Self ↔ Higher (Transpersonal)

Covered in `_core/Daily Architecture.md` (Phase 4: Dissolve).

- **Awe Walk** (Keltner) — disengages the Default Mode Network, "small-self" effect.
- **Service check** (Frankl) — "Who was I useful to today?" Shift focus from consumption to contribution.

**Idol of Spiritual Ego** — using practices to amplify the "I" instead of dissolving it.

## Daily protocol (operational)

The hourly version of all this lives in `_core/Daily Architecture.md`:
- **Phase 1 (morning):** Self ↔ Self — meditation, system debug.
- **Phase 2 (work):** Self ↔ Work — Deep Work, DMN reset.
- **Phase 3 (evening):** Self ↔ Others — Connection Mode (phone away, Turn Towards).
- **Phase 4 (night):** Self ↔ Higher — Awe micro-dose, service check, NSDR.

## Inversions

- "I do everything for the family" (work, earn, train) without Turn Towards — investment without presence violates Magic Ratio. The partner doesn't feel cared for; they feel managed.
- Turning every conversation into a lecture (Power) or a demonstration of achievement (Fame).
- "Kids are still young, I'll have time" — Tail End says otherwise. 93% is already behind by their 18.
- Postponing a call to parents ("tomorrow") — at 2-3 visits per year, each skipped call ≈ -3% of the remaining lifetime budget with them.
- Believing that a meditation streak proves anything about Level 1. Self ↔ Self is necessary, not sufficient.

## Defcon collapse

| Level | Relationship rule kept |
|:---|:---|
| **Level 2 (Normal)** | All four loops active per Daily Architecture. |
| **Level 1 (Low Power)** | Connection Mode survives — even shortened. Better 30 min full presence than 3 h "next to but in the phone". |
| **Level 0 (Safe Mode)** | Just don't break Magic Ratio. One short Turn Towards bid per day is enough to keep the channel alive. |

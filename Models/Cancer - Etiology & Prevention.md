# Cancer — Etiology & Prevention

Half of a two-model pair (detection side: `Cancer - Screening & Treatment.md`). The **cause → prevention** lens, collapsing the field to one action-question: *is this cancer **modifiable**, or **bad luck**?*

## Mechanism

### Two buckets by cause
- **Bad luck (unpreventable):** random replication-error mutations and/or germline predisposition — e.g. most pancreatic adenocarcinoma. Arrives like a bomb through the roof; the average-risk person can't meaningfully lower the odds or "prepare."
- **Modifiable (oncogenic-factor driven):** cumulative exposure to promoters raises lifetime risk. Population-dominant modifiable causes, in order: **smoking ≫ adiposity/metabolic, alcohol, chronic infections (HPV, H. pylori, HBV/HCV, EBV), UV, ionizing radiation, specific dietary/occupational exposures.**

### The insulin / IGF-1 axis
A real driver — insulin and IGF-1 are mitogenic and anti-apoptotic; chronic hyperinsulinemia (the *AUC* of insulin over years) is mechanistically tied to colorectal, endometrial, pancreatic, breast and other cancers, and is a large part of *how* adiposity raises risk. It is **not** "the single strongest oncogen" (smoking dwarfs it) — but once smoking/alcohol are removed it is the **top residual modifiable lever**: lower chronic insulin/glucose exposure → lower promotion.

### Germline predisposition (the inherited part of the "bad luck" bucket)
- **Germline** = the DNA you're born with, in every cell, inherited from parents and heritable to children. Pathogenic high-penetrance mutations (BRCA1/2; mismatch-repair / Lynch: MLH1/MSH2/MSH6/PMS2/EPCAM; APC, MUTYH, CDH1, TP53) raise baseline risk from birth.
- **≠ common-SNP polygenic risk** (low-penetrance rsID "risk scores") and **≠ somatic** mutations (acquired in a tumor, tissue-only, guide *treatment* not heredity). A consumer common-SNP report does **not** establish germline predisposition status.
- **Practical hinge:** a **strong family history** — especially a same-organ or Lynch-spectrum cluster (colorectal + gastric + endometrial) — is the flag to *establish germline status*, because a positive result **converts a "bird" into a surveillable target** (moves the person into the intensified-screening protocol — see the screening model).

## Scale of simplicity

Cancer etiology at the molecular scale (driver mutations, pathways per tumor) is intractable and yields no life action. This model coarse-grains to **one 2-way question — modifiable or luck?** At that scale "what do I *do* about causing cancer?" collapses to: **pull the few real modifiable levers; for the luck bucket, don't chase prevention that doesn't exist — but first check whether inherited risk makes it *not* luck.**

**Usefulness test:** drop to tumor biology and it's complex with no personal prevention action — below-scale. Stay at the lever/heredity level.

## Cynefin domain

- **Mechanism (carcinogen → risk):** **Complicated** for established causes (smoking, HPV, UV, H. pylori) — ordered, causal. **Complex/contested** for the insulin axis at the outcome level (mechanism solid, hard-endpoint modification unproven — see Falsification).
- **Outcome (does *this* person get cancer):** **Complex** — emergent from genes × exposures × chance. A population lever is never a personal guarantee.

## Time horizon (Lyapunov)

Prevention integrates over **decades** — direction stable (lifelong low insulin / no smoking → lower risk), magnitude for any individual not forecastable. **Germline risk is lifelong and fixed from birth** — test once, it never changes.

## Practice

- **Don't smoke; keep alcohol low; stay lean / low chronic insulin** (glucose + insulin AUC); **UV / skin protection.**
- **Clear the oncogenic infections:** HPV vaccine; test/treat **H. pylori**; HBV vaccine.
- **Map family history once;** where it warrants (strong / early-onset / clustered), **establish germline status** — decision-changing because it reclassifies the screening protocol.

## Confidence

- **High:** the two-bucket framing; smoking as #1; infections / UV as established causal levers.
- **Medium:** insulin's *quantitative* weight, and that lowering it modifies hard cancer endpoints (mechanism strong, RCT proof weak).
- **Low / contested:** the exact split between "bad luck" and "modifiable."

## Falsification

**Attack 1 — "insulin is the dominant modifiable lever."** Counter: **metformin** (lowers insulin) has **not** delivered clean cancer-prevention wins in RCTs; Mendelian randomization on T2D/insulin and most cancers is mixed. → **Qualified, downgraded:** insulin is real and the top *residual* lever once smoking/alcohol are gone, but "strongest oncogen" fails and "lower insulin → fewer cancers" is not RCT-proven. Kept as mechanism-plus-association, not causal certainty.

**Attack 2 — "bad luck is a big, fixed bucket."** Counter: **Tomasetti–Vogelstein (2015/2017)** argued most cancer is random replication errors; **heavily contested** (Wu et al. 2016: ~70–90% of risk has an environmental / modifiable contribution). → **Qualified:** the bad-luck bucket is **real but smaller than intuition** — don't over-assign to luck and under-invest in prevention.

**Result — survives, both claims qualified.** Directs prevention correctly (pull real levers, don't fatalistically write off modifiable risk, check heredity) provided insulin is held as high-leverage-not-proven and luck as smaller-than-it-feels.

## Boundaries

- **Not diagnosis / screening / treatment** — the detection side is the paired model.
- **Population / heuristic level** — cannot predict who gets cancer.
- **Insulin branch is mechanism + association, not RCT-causal** — don't oversell it (contrast `ApoB and Atherosclerosis.md`, which *is* causal).
- **Ignores** hereditary-syndrome detail, the tumor microenvironment, staging.

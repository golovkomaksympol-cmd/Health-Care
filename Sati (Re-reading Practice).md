# Sati — the Practice of Re-reading

The meta-practice that keeps the system from drifting. Not the same as Architect mode (re-planning) or Operator mode (executing). Sati is **reload of the config from source of truth**.

## Why it's needed

Between Architect mode (every ~2 weeks: question and re-plan) and Operator mode (daily: execute without thinking), **config drift** accumulates. The Operator forgets *why* they're doing what they're doing, slides into old patterns, loses the felt sense of identity behind the system.

The drift is invisible from inside — that's its whole problem. The Operator believes the system is running because they're still doing the actions; meanwhile the *meaning* of the actions has detached from the original intent.

Sati is the bridge: re-reading the source-of-truth document not to edit it, not to analyze it, but to remember.

## Traditional analogues

The pattern shows up in every contemplative tradition under different names:

- **Sati** (Pali) — literally "remembrance". Not "be in the moment" — *remember the intention, remember the teaching*.
- **Taking Refuge** (Buddhism) — daily reload of orientation: who am I, where am I going, what do I lean on.
- **Hypomnemata** (Stoics) — personal notebooks of principles, for re-reading. Marcus Aurelius's *Meditations* is exactly this.
- **Shema Israel / Creed** — daily recitation of an identity-statement.
- **Anamnesis** (Plato) — "non-forgetting": we don't *learn* truth, we *remember* it.

The pattern is the same: regular reload of identity and orientation, by repetition, not by analysis.

## Protocol

- **Regular (1-2×/week):** Re-read the patient's own `Medical Profile.md` and `Human Context.md` in full, or selected sections. Don't analyze. Don't edit. Just remember.
- **On drift:** If the patient notices they've "forgotten" why X — re-read that section. (Examples: "why don't I drink alcohol", "why this specific morning protocol", "why this is on the STOP-list".)
- **In crisis (Defcon 0):** Re-read only the Level-0 sections — Sleep, Alcohol, Sugar, anything else flagged Defcon 0. Three things, no more.
- **In Architect mode (every 2 weeks):** Re-read **plus** edit. This is no longer Sati — it's Architect. Don't conflate them.

## Why it's Defcon 0

Counterintuitive but real: Sati belongs at Defcon 0 precisely because it's needed most in crisis. When everything is falling apart, re-reading the Defcon-0 practices = remembering what to do. It's the emergency-instruction sheet that only works if read **before** or **during** the emergency.

## Inversions (how to break it)

- "I remember it all already" — config drift is invisible from inside. That's the whole reason a reload from disk is needed.
- Turning Sati into Architect mode ("oh, this needs fixing") — one is re-reading, the other is editing. Do not conflate.
- Stopping the re-reading because "the system is working" — the system is working *because* of the re-reading.

# Daily Architecture

The shape of a "good day" — four phases. Operating environment: 4 levels of relationship (Self, Work, Others, Higher). Each phase has an "idol" to guard against.

This is the **architecture**, not a script. Patients adapt timing to their chronotype and life shape; the phase order and the idol-guards are invariant.

## Phase 1: Load
**Loop: Self ↔ Self. Idol to guard: Pleasure.**

- **T+0 — Cortisol trigger.** Wake + immediate sunlight in the eyes (2-10 min). Synchronizes the circadian clock.
- **T+15 min — System Debug.**
    - *Mindfulness:* 10 min observing consciousness. Goal: notice the "background noise" (anxiety / irritation) and dis-identify from it.
- **T+30 min — Input Block.** No "read-only" mode. No news, no social media. The mind hasn't loaded its agency layer yet — feeding it inputs in this window installs other people's priorities as your own.

## Phase 2: High Performance
**Loop: Self ↔ Work. Idols to guard: Money, Power.**

- **3 hours/day — Deep Work session (Cal Newport).**
    - *Environment:* phone physically removed or in airplane mode.
    - *Protocol:* one cognitively hard task.
    - *Flow check (Csikszentmihalyi):* bored → make it harder (set a tight deadline). Anxious → decompose.
    - *Friction (Huberman):* welcome the initial resistance as the noradrenaline signature. Don't seek easy dopamine in the first 10 minutes.
- **Midday — DMN Reset.**
    - Walk or eat **without content** (no podcasts / YouTube).
    - Let the Default Mode Network process the morning's data. This is when consolidation and insight happen — not during focused work.

## Phase 3: Sync
**Loop: Self ↔ Others. Idol to guard: Fame (recognition-seeking).**

- **End of work day — Shutdown Ritual.** Close laptop. Trigger phrase: "Work cycle complete." Hard separation of "Work" and "Home" contexts.
- **Evening — Connection Mode (Gottman).**
    - *Hardware lock:* phone in a drawer ("Station to Station").
    - *Turn-Towards protocol:* physical orientation to partner / family. Max priority to any incoming bid.
    - *Audit:* Am I trying to impress (Fame) or to teach (Power)? Switch to listening.

## Phase 4: Dissolve
**Loop: Self ↔ Higher. Idol to guard: Spiritual Ego.**

- **T-1h from sleep — Blue Light Filter.** Reduce screen brightness, dim lights (melatonin prep).
- **T-30 min — Transpersonal Exit.**
    - *Awe micro-dose:* look at the night sky, listen to complex music, read "deep time" literature. Feel a scale larger than self.
    - *Service check:* the day's question: "Who was I useful to?" Shift focus from consumption to contribution.
- **T-0 — Sleep Protocol (NSDR).** Yoga nidra or body scan for full relaxation.

## Why the architecture, not a script

The phase order matters more than the exact times. Some patients run Phase 1 at 06:00, some at 10:30 — the work is the same. The architecture also collapses gracefully under Defcon level shifts:

| Level | What survives |
|:---|:---|
| **Level 2 (Normal)** | All four phases. |
| **Level 1 (Low Power)** | Phase 1 (Input Block) + Phase 3 (Connection Mode, even if shortened) + Phase 4 (Sleep Protocol). Drop Deep Work. |
| **Level 0 (Safe Mode)** | Sleep Protocol. Phone away. That's it. |

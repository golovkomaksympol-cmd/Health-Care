# Health Philosophy

The "why" layer behind every decision in this repo.

## CEO mindset

Treat doctors and experts as consultants/contractors. The patient owns final decisions and outcomes. The framework's job is to give them the data and reasoning to do that well.

## Preventive & personalized (Medicine 3.0)

Don't wait for breakdowns (Medicine 2.0). Prefer **predictive maintenance** based on genetics, individual risk factors, and biochemistry. Catch trajectories early when interventions are cheap.

## Health 3.0 goal

Not "absence of disease" — **maximize healthspan** (quality years). A 90-year-old who can lift a grandchild, hike a hill, and decide for themselves is the success metric.

## Strategic focus: the Four Horsemen defense

Almost everything that kills or disables people late in life falls into four buckets. Focus prevention budget here; everything else is noise.

1. **Cardiovascular disease** (atherosclerosis)
2. **Neurodegeneration** (Alzheimer's, Parkinson's)
3. **Metabolic dysfunction** (T2D, fatty liver, sarcopenic obesity)
4. **Cancer**

Causal upstream metrics matter more than symptoms: ApoB > total cholesterol; fasting insulin > fasting glucose; visceral fat (DXA) > BMI; VO2 max > "how the workout felt".

## Reverse engineering (Centenarian Decathlon)

Plan backwards from the "90-year-old self":

- Define the physical / cognitive / social capacities required at 90.
- Estimate the buffer needed to still have them after age-related decline.
- Build that buffer **now**, while it's cheap to build and compounds.

Typical capacities to define:
- Carry [X] kg up [N] flights of stairs
- Hike [X] km with [Y] m elevation in a day
- Lift a grandchild
- Full self-sufficiency in daily life (incl. fine motor tasks)
- Preserve mental clarity and emotional stability
- Wake up content with the day ahead

The specific list belongs in each patient's `Human Context.md`; the principle of working backwards from end-state capacities belongs here.

## What this philosophy explicitly rejects

- Reactive medicine (wait until a symptom shows up, then treat).
- Population-average targets when individual data is available.
- "Risk factor accumulation" framing without a decision rule attached.
- Spending attention budget on novelty supplements before sleep / movement / nutrition / strength are solid.

# Nutrition Framework

The substrate of metabolic health. Defaults below; patient-specific tuning (allergies, intolerances, diagnoses, current macros) belongs in their `Medical Profile.md`.

## Eating window (time-restricted feeding)

| Parameter | Value | Why |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Minimum gap between dinner and breakfast** | **12 h** | The threshold where the switch from glucose to fat oxidation (lipolysis) starts. Important at HOMA-IR ≥ 1.5. |
| **Optimum** | **14 h** | Peak fat oxidation and basal insulin reduction. |
| **Maximum (occasional)** | **16 h** | Don't run this daily if ferritin < 30 or training load is high — dose-dependent fatigue. |

**Important**: 14h of fasting does **not** mean skipping breakfast. It means moving dinner earlier. A late dinner (after 20:00) is worse than an early breakfast.

## Protein

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Target** | **1.2-1.6 g/kg/day** | Higher than the "standard" 0.8 g/kg, because: (1) protects lean mass under caloric deficit, (2) thermic effect ~25% (burns calories on absorption), (3) satiety. For older patients or active strength training, push toward the upper end. |
| **Distribution** | 3 meals × 25-35 g | Per-meal absorption cap ~40 g. Leucine threshold for MPS activation is ~2.5 g leucine ≈ 25-30 g animal protein. |
| **Meal order** | 1) Fiber (vegetables) → 2) Protein/fat → 3) Carbs last | Slows gastric emptying; cuts post-prandial glucose peak 30-40%. |

### Protein supplementation
- Whey isolate or casein for fill-the-gap when food doesn't reach the daily target.
- Don't replace a real meal (especially dinner) with a shake — solids carry the rest of what the meal is doing.

## Other macros and food categories

- **Fiber**: every meal, ~½ plate vegetables/greens.
- **Fats**: ~0.8-1.0 g/kg/day. Prioritize monounsaturated (olive oil, avocado, nuts). Limit saturated when ApoB is elevated.
- **Fructose**: limit. No juice, no smoothies, no dried fruit. Berries are fine (blueberry, raspberry, strawberry).
- **Alcohol**: 0 if autoimmune condition present (Hashimoto, RA, etc.) or fatty liver flagged. Otherwise minimize — there is no safe dose for long-term health; the question is acceptable cost.
- **Ultra-processed food**: minimize regardless of calorie count. The processing itself disrupts satiety signals and microbiome.

## Glucose management practices

- **Walk after meals** (10-15 min). Mechanism: muscle contraction → GLUT4 translocation → glucose utilized bypassing insulin. Visible on CGM as flattened post-prandial peak.
- **Fiber first** at every meal (see meal order above).

## Defcon collapse

| Level | Nutrition rule kept |
|:---|:---|
| **Level 2 (Normal)** | Full framework above. |
| **Level 1 (Low Power)** | Protein at every meal; fiber at every meal; no alcohol; no sugar. Drop fasting precision, drop CGM-driven tweaks. |
| **Level 0 (Safe Mode)** | Just no toxic input: no alcohol, no sugar, no junk. Eat when hungry, sleep as priority. |

# Training Framework

Weekly skeleton and details for the four pillars of physical training: core stability, strength, Zone 2, Zone 5.

## Weekly skeleton (default)

- **Daily**: McGill Big 3 +1 (8–10 min)
- **Strength**: 3×/week, 45–60 min, A/B/A → B/A/B alternating
- **Zone 2**: 3×/week, 60+ min/session (can substitute 1-2×/week with a sport of choice)
- **Zone 5 (HIIT)**: 1×/week, Norwegian 4×4

Total active time: ~6-8h/week. Defcon Level 2 (Normal Operation).

## 1. Stability / mobility / posture micro-practices

### Micro-breaks at the desk
- Brugger's Postural Relief Position every ~30 min: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-U2NxmqOSGU

### "Movement as hygiene" (short complex)
- Jeff Cavaliere "time under tension" idea: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGsS-T-cSEc
- Variants: bear crawl, reverse crab, inchworm (standing → plank)

### Foot
- Toe yoga + short-foot drill: brief, regular

## 2. Daily core: McGill Big 3 +1

Purpose: build core stability (especially against hyperlordosis / sedentary work) without overloading the lumbar spine.

- **McGill Curl-up (modified)**: 6 × 10 sec, 3 sec rest, alternating leg.
- **Side Plank**: 6 × 10 sec per side.
- **Bird-Dog**: 12 × 10 sec, alternating sides.
- **Glute Bridge (the "+1")**: 2 × 30 sec with hard glute squeeze, neutral pelvis, no lumbar arching.

## 3. Strength (45-60 min)

### Warm-up (5-10 min)
- Active joint rotations (arms, hips, leg swings).
- 1 short core set (McGill Big 3) as activation.
- 1 warm-up set at lower weight per main lift.

### Loading principles
- Pick weight such that **~12-15 reps to failure** (or close to it).
- **Last 5 reps** are the ones that matter — they're the growth stimulus.
- **Eccentric (lowering) slow** — 3-4 sec; concentric explosive but controlled.
- Volume target: **~10 working sets per muscle group per week**.

### Weekly pattern
- 3 sessions per week, template **A / B / A** one week, **B / A / B** the next.
- Each session = a "menu without thinking": warm-up → 5-7 exercises → finishers.

### Session A (Full body — squat/press + pull)
1. **Squat variant**: back squat / front squat / goblet squat
2. **Press variant**: bench press / incline DB press / weighted push-ups
3. **Hinge variant**: Romanian deadlift / deadlift (moderate) / hip hinge
4. **Vertical pull**: pull-ups / lat pulldown
5. **Horizontal pull**: row (cable/DB/bar)
6. (optional) **Single-leg**: split squat / lunge

**Sets/reps default**: 2-4 working sets, ~8-12 reps, close to failure. Rest 60-120 sec. Technique > weight.

### Session B (Full body — hinge/overhead press + squat)
1. **Hinge variant**: deadlift (moderate) / RDL / hip hinge
2. **Overhead press**: bar / DB
3. **Knee-dominant squat**: front squat / goblet squat / leg press
4. **Vertical pull**: pull-ups / lat pulldown
5. **Horizontal pull**: row
6. (optional) **Glutes**: hip thrust / glute bridge (heavier than McGill+1 version)

### Finishers (end of strength session)
- **Suitcase Carry**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89Ojpz5GuLM
- **Face Pulls**: 2-4 sets, scapular control, no cheating.

## 4. Zone 2 (mitochondrial / metabolic base)

- 3-4×/week
- **60+ minutes** per session
- Formats: running, cycling, elliptical, rowing, nordic walking — anything sustainable for an hour.
- Criterion: stable low intensity, no pauses. Talk test — can speak in full sentences.
- "Nose-breathing test": if forced to mouth-breathe, you're too fast.
- The first ~30 min are mostly glycogen burn; the real Zone 2 minutes accumulate after that. Don't cut sessions short.

## 5. Zone 5 (HIIT / cardiac output / VO2 max)

- 1×/week
- **Norwegian 4×4**: 4 min at 90-95% HRmax / 4 min easy recovery × 4 cycles.
- Total session ~30 min including warm-up/cool-down.
- The one cardio session you don't skip if you have to drop everything else.

## Defcon collapse (graceful degradation)

| Level | Training preserved |
|:---|:---|
| **Level 2 (Normal)** | Full schedule above. |
| **Level 1 (Low Power)** | McGill Big 3 → 1 set of plank. Strength → 1 compound lift, 2 sets. Zone 2 → 30-min walk. Drop Zone 5. |
| **Level 0 (Safe Mode)** | Sleep + 10-min walk if possible. Nothing else. |

## Patient-specific tuning lives in their Medical Profile

If a patient has a hernia, joint limit, or pregnancy contra-indication, the modification belongs in their `Medical Profile.md`, not here. This file is the unencumbered baseline.
